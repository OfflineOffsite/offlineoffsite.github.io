01 Primary(How It Works)
HEADER How It Works
SUBHEADER Five steps, entirely by mail.
BODY 1. Encrypt your drive. We strongly recommend encrypting your device and files before you ship them. Your data is your responsibility.
BODY 2. Download and print the form. Use the button at the top of this page to print the OfflineOffsite mail-in form, then fill it out by hand.
BODY 3. Pack your parcel. Place your device, the printed form, cash for payment, and a prepaid USPS return-address label inside an accepted USPS Priority Mail Flat-Rate box or envelope.
BODY 4. Mail it to us. Send your parcel to the address in the Contact section.
BODY 5. We store it, then return it. We keep your drive offline and offsite for your chosen term, then ship it back using the USPS return label you provided.
NOTE We work exclusively with USPS because USPS return labels remain valid for up to 365 days — longer than any other major carrier.
