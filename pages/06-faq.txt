FAQ
SUBHEADER(L)
Why USPS only?
BODY
USPS return-address labels stay valid for up to 365 days, far longer than other carriers offer. That lets us hold your drive and still return it on the label you sent.
SUBHEADER(L)
Do you keep any of my data or personal information?
BODY
No. We collect no personal information, create no accounts, and never access the contents of your device.
SUBHEADER(L)
What if my drive fails in storage?
BODY
We store media offline and untouched, but we cannot guarantee hardware. Encrypt your data and keep an independent backup. See Encryption & Responsibility.
SUBHEADER(L)
How do I get my device back?
BODY
We ship it to you using the prepaid USPS return-address label you included with your parcel.
